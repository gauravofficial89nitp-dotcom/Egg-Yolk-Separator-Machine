# Egg Yolk Separator Machine

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Egg Yolk Separator Machine** is a mechanical design project focused on developing a simple and efficient mechanism for separating egg yolk from egg white. The machine is designed to reduce manual effort and improve the consistency and efficiency of the separation process.

The project involves the **3D CAD modeling, mechanism development, and assembly design** of the machine, with emphasis on simplicity, functionality, ease of operation, and manufacturability.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Design a practical mechanism for separating egg yolk and egg white.
* Develop detailed 3D CAD models of the machine components.
* Reduce manual effort involved in the separation process.
* Improve the consistency and efficiency of yolk separation.
* Apply mechanical design and mechanism principles to a real-world application.

## ⚙️ Major Components

* Supporting Frame
* Egg Holding Mechanism
* Egg Breaking/Opening Mechanism
* Yolk Separation Mechanism
* Egg White Collection Tray
* Yolk Collection Container
* Lever/Actuation Mechanism
* Connecting and Supporting Components

## 🔧 Working Principle

The egg is positioned in the designated holding mechanism and opened using the designed mechanism. The geometry and movement of the separator allow the **egg white to pass into a separate collection section while retaining the yolk**. The separated yolk is then collected in a dedicated container.

## 🛠️ Design & Development

The machine was developed using CAD-based mechanical design techniques. Individual components were modeled and assembled to evaluate their relative positioning, movement, and integration. The design emphasizes:

* Simple mechanical operation
* Efficient component arrangement
* Ease of manufacturing and assembly
* Compact and practical construction
* Reduced manual intervention
